# Prompts das imagens finais

Imagens criadas com a geração integrada de imagens, usando as referências apenas como orientação visual. Os arquivos estão em `assets/quarto-punk.png` e `assets/avatar-punk.png`.

## Banner

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original 3:1 panoramic banner for a GitHub profile, 1920x640.
Input images: the three attached images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: learn bold flat ink shapes and angular manga contour from reference 1, torn-paper DIY punk print construction from reference 2, urban graffiti energy from reference 3. DO NOT copy any character, slogan, logo, symbol, layout or exact composition.
Primary request: a cozy late-1980s / mid-1990s developer's improvised bedroom-workshop, with a much stronger PUNK ZINE and graffiti identity. A developer who brought an old computer room into the present. Simple, readable and carefully composed.
Scene: wide intimate view of a small room. At left-center, a chunky beige CRT and old keyboard on a battered wooden desk, a dark olive screen with a tiny warm cream cursor only. A patched rust-colored office chair faces the desk, a worn bomber jacket draped over its back. Warm anglepoise lamp, coffee mug, two floppy disks, a little cassette player, one small potted plant. Small spray can and screwdriver on the desk. The wall behind has an ORIGINAL broad abstract graffiti mural formed from a few black angular sweeping shapes and muted salmon areas; NOT letters or words, no eye symbol. Two torn blank cream/salmon scraps held with tape. At right a simple window reveals a few rooftops at golden hour. Sense of an inhabited space, no people.
Style/medium: distinctly 2D hand-inked manga-inspired editorial illustration fused with underground 90s skate zine and screen print. Strong thick tapered black contours, large flat shadow shapes, restrained halftone ONLY inside a few shaded surfaces. Warm flat gouache fills. Deliberate geometric silhouettes. Fewer but stronger details. Slightly crooked perspective and handmade feeling. NOT photorealism, NOT 3D, NOT painterly realism.
Composition: continuous edge-to-edge landscape, one scene, desk and monitor prominent left/middle, graffiti wall and window balance right. Keep readable at 900px width. Clear focal hierarchy and breathing space, no collage of tiny panels.
Palette: warm aged cream, nearly black ink, dusty salmon pink, muted mustard and tobacco rust, very restrained dark olive for CRT. The warmth comes from ochre lamplight and afternoon sun.
Text: none.
Constraints: no personal name, no text/letters/words/numbers, no signatures, no watermark, no copied characters, no political slogans, no luxury branding. No dreamcore portal, no neon synthwave, no purple gradient. Avoid dense fine scratches, webby crosshatching, gratuitous cables, busy ornament and random details. Cozy, punk, understated, urban.
```

## Avatar

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: square optional GitHub avatar, 1024x1024.
Reference images are inspiration for broad flat colors, angular black manga contours, DIY punk zine and graffiti energy only. Do not copy existing characters or logos.
Primary request: an original personless emblem: an old beige CRT monitor customized with a single strip of blank pink tape, one jagged black lightning-bolt decal on its casing, a little bent silver safety pin pierced through the tape. On screen is one bold cream angular four-point spark on a muted black-olive field. Simple, memorable, slightly rebellious and cozy.
Composition: central oversized monitor in a slightly tilted three-quarter front view, thick dynamic black outlines, generous quiet aged-cream margin around the entire object for a circular avatar crop. One broad dusty salmon offset block behind the monitor, no extra props, no frame.
Style: 2D underground 90s skate sticker, manga ink with chunky flat shadows, screen-printed spot colors. Hand-drawn, graphic and readable at tiny scale. Very restrained patchy ink texture only, no fine crosshatching. Use a warm cream, dusty salmon, near-black and mustard palette.
Constraints: no face, no person, absolutely no words, letters, numbers, names, watermarks or logos. No dream landscape. No 3D rendering, no realistic shading. Avoid excessive scratch marks, noise or complex ornament.
```
